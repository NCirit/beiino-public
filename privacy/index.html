Beiino

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Beiino game (package com.ncirit.beiino, “the game”) handles. The game is made by Nurullah Cirit (“we”, “us”). If you have questions, email nma.cirit@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You can play without an account. Your progress is saved on your device.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Some features use Google services (Play Games, AdMob ads, Firebase Analytics, Google Play purchases). Each one is described below, including what it collects and how to turn it off.

1. Data stored on your device

Your scores, levels, medals, daily results, chosen animal and settings are saved in the game's private storage on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Google Play Games Services

If you use Google Play Games, the game uses it for achievements, leaderboards and to save a copy of your progress to your Google account, so you can continue on another device. This uses your Play Games player ID and game progress (scores, levels, medals, settings). Leaderboard scores and your Play Games profile name may be visible to other players according to your Play Games settings. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete the cloud save from the game's settings, and manage Play Games data in your Google account.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version can show ads served by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about ad interactions and app diagnostics.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads or analytics use your data, through Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Buying “Remove ads” turns off ads that you did not choose to watch. More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example which levels are too hard or where players stop, so we can improve it. It collects an app instance identifier, device and app information (model, Android version, language, country) and game events (such as rounds played, levels reached and settings used). It does not collect your name, email or contacts. You can turn analytics off in the game's settings. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. The game receives a purchase confirmation so it can unlock your item and restore it later. We never see your payment details. Google’s handling of payment data is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Sharing your daily result

When you share a Daily Fold result, the game passes a short text (date, score and an emoji line) to the Android share menu. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app to share with.

7. Permissions

The game uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback and, once ads or online features are enabled, internet access. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and data deletion

  • Turn off personalised ads and analytics in Settings → Privacy options (where available) and the analytics switch.
  • Delete your cloud save in the game's settings, or ask us at nma.cirit@gmail.com.
  • Delete everything on your device by uninstalling the game.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Email us to use them. We do not sell personal information.

10. How to delete your data

  • Progress on your device: uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Beiino → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Cloud save: in the game, open Settings → Delete cloud save, or remove Beiino’s data from your Play Games account in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.
  • Ads and analytics data: reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. To ask us to delete analytics data associated with your device, email nma.cirit@gmail.com with the subject “Beiino data deletion”. We reply within 30 days. Analytics data is otherwise deleted automatically after 14 months.
  • Purchases: purchase records are kept by Google Play as required by law; you can view them in your Google Play order history.
